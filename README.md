# Citation Clinic

Two short, game-based tutorials that teach health sciences students how to cite and reference.

| Tutorial | Style | File |
|---|---|---|
| APA Citation Clinic | APA 7th edition | `apa-citation-clinic.html` |
| AMA Citation Clinic | AMA 11th edition | `ama-citation-clinic.html` |

`index.html` is a landing page that links to both.

## How the tutorials work

- Each tutorial has four short modules. Every module ends with a 10-question game.
- A student needs 9 out of 10 to unlock the next module. After a miss, they redo only the questions they got wrong, and the answer stays hidden until they get it right.
- After the final rounds, the student confirms their name and downloads a certificate of completion with a verification code.
- Progress is saved in the student's own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Publishing with GitHub Pages

1. In the repository, open **Settings**, then **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set the source to **Deploy from a branch**, choose the `main` branch and the root folder, and save.
3. After a minute or two the site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.

To update a tutorial, upload the new version of its `.html` file over the old one.
